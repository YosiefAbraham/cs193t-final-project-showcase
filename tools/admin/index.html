    Each topic is a chip on the showcase and a search shortcut. Add the words people actually type, like gym, meal prep or budget. Phrases win over single words, so “work out” finds Fitness before “work” finds Work & career.

    Put it on the course site
    
            
    From the cs193t-showcase folder, install it into the course’s Hugo site. This copies showcase/ to static/final_project/showcase/, removing files of students who withdrew.
